Palette: cozy, not sugary

Cozy not sugary: joy colours are saturated but always carry a little brown or grey, so they glow against the warm greys instead of shouting. No neon, no candy pastels. Shadows lean dusty violet, light leans honey, and the grey world is warm, never blue-cold, so it reads as joyless-but-gentle rather than hostile.
